Research notes

Data and scope

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators, using the cleaned country-year CSV committed to this repository. Coverage is 2000–2023 and varies by indicator. Health spending uses current international dollars at purchasing-power parity; these are not constant-price amounts.

Interpretation

Correlations across countries do not establish that additional health spending causes longer lives. Income, population health, institutions, demographics, epidemics and other factors can confound the relationship. The original R scripts in the repository contain the econometric models, Malawi–Rwanda case study and experimental Health Performance Index; this dashboard does not substitute for those analyses.
